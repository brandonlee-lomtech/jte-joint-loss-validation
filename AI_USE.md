# AI-use disclosure

The Challenge asks teams to disclose any AI use. This is our disclosure for this repository.

| Area | AI involvement | Human responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Rules and brief analysis | An AI assistant (Grok Bot, an AI agent product used by LOMTECH GLOBAL Co., Ltd.) extracted and summarised the public Challenge pages, Rules and Detailed Challenge Brief. | The team checked the extract against the official pages. |
| Code (`src/jlref/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `team_solver.py`, `run_all.sh`) | First draft written by the AI assistant under team direction, then run and tested in this repository. The reference solver is a simplified, two-country version of our engine. | The team owns, reviews and is accountable for the code. Every number in `results/` is regenerated by `run_all.sh`. |
| Numbers | All numbers in `results/` come from executed code, never from generated text. Baselines come from the unmodified official `qjlb` package. Numbers in `REPORTED_RESULTS.md` are copied verbatim from the full engine's result tables and logs. | The team verified the solver against the official exact solver (`qjlb validate`, `tests/`). |
| Documentation (README, this file, `REPORTED_RESULTS.md`) | First draft by the AI assistant. | The team edited and approved the text. |
| Quantum execution | None in this repository. | — |

No confidential third-party data was given to AI tools. The benchmark data is public (ADB `qjlb` benchmark, Apache-2.0; World Bank data, CC BY 4.0).
